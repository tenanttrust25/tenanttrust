Common questions

Is this legal advice?

No. We provide administrative help — preparing and serving documents — not legal advice, and we don't represent anyone before the Landlord and Tenant Board. For a hearing, you'll want a licensed paralegal.

Do you do evictions / LTB filings?

We prepare and serve the notices. Filing applications at the LTB and appearing at hearings requires a licence, so we refer that to a licensed paralegal.

What if I just want the free tools?

Use them as much as you like — tenanttrust.ca/tools. This page is only for people who'd rather not do the paperwork themselves.
