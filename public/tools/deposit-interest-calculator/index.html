Last Month's Rent Deposit — Interest Calculator

In Ontario a landlord owes annual interest on the last month's rent deposit. See what's owed and whether the deposit is even legal.

The rules in a nutshell

  • The only deposit a landlord may collect is a last month's rent deposit — no damage deposits, no key deposits.
  • It cannot be more than one month's rent (one week for a weekly tenancy).
  • The landlord must pay interest every year equal to the rent increase guideline, applied to the deposit — credited against rent or paid to the tenant.
  • On move-out the deposit covers the last month; any interest is accounted for separately.
