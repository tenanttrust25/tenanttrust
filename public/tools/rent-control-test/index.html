Is My Unit Rent-Controlled?

The Nov 15, 2018 test decides whether the rent increase guideline caps your increases (Form N1) or your unit is exempt (Form N2).

YesSomeone lived there on/before Nov 15, 2018
NoFirst occupied after Nov 15, 2018
Not sure
YesThe house had ≤2 units before that date, the owner lived in another unit (or it was an unfinished space like a basement when first occupied), and it was created after Nov 15, 2018
No / not all of it

In plain terms

Units first occupied after Nov 15, 2018 (new buildings, new additions, most new basement units) are usually exempt: any increase is allowed on Form N2, though the 90-day notice and 12-month rules still apply. Everything older is rent-controlled (Form N1). If the exemption is disputed, the landlord must prove it.
